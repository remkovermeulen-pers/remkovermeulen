Healthcare Technology  /  Case Study

Koa Health

Building the digital infrastructure for proactive mental wellbeing through machine learning and human-centric design.

Tech Stack

Python React AWS PyTorch
Role

VP & Head of Product

Timeline

2019 — 2023

Partners

01

The Challenge

Mental health support is traditionally reactive. Koa Health sought to transform this paradigm by leveraging large-scale data to predict crises before they occur. The architectural challenge lay in designing a system that felt clinically robust yet fundamentally human.

The primary hurdle was the ethical integration of machine learning — creating a transparent interface that empowered clinicians rather than alienating them with "black box" algorithms. We needed to ensure that data privacy was paramount while maintaining high utility.

02

Methodology

Our approach centred on "Evidence-Based Design," a process where every UI component is validated against clinical safety standards. We conducted extensive ethnographic research with crisis response teams to map the high-stress decision-making journey.

Phase I  ·  Data Audit

Mapping over 400 unique data points from disparate clinical sources into a unified health record schema.

Phase II  ·  Stress Testing

Simulating high-pressure triage environments to measure cognitive load and response time latency.

By treating the interface as a medical instrument, we moved beyond typical SaaS patterns toward a highly specialised environment tailored for mental health specialists.

03

The Solution

We developed a multi-layered interface system that prioritises data hierarchy. By utilising a "Soft Brutalist" aesthetic, we established a sense of structural permanence and clinical honesty.

The dashboard features a modular grid that allows clinicians to customise views based on patient severity scores, integrating real-time prediction markers directly into the workflow.

Technical innovation was pushed further through the development of an "Interpretability Layer" — a unique UI component that explains the 'why' behind an AI-driven prediction, citing the specific clinical variables that triggered an alert.

04

Impact

Post-deployment metrics showed a significant reduction in administrative overhead for practitioners, allowing for a 30% increase in patient-facing time. The predictive engine maintained an 80% recall rate during the first six months of clinical trials.

Beyond the numbers, the project transformed the internal culture at Koa Health, establishing a design-led framework for future product iterations. This project successfully bridged the gap between advanced data science and practical healthcare application.
